# Chat Core Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Add private, invite-and-approve text chats inside spaces on the relay, the PC app and the phone app. Chats support links, editing, deleting, admin moderation, unread counts and notifications.

**Architecture:**
- **Relay:** chats, members, invites and messages become SQLite tables next to spaces. Each chat has a revision counter `rev` that is bumped on every change. Clients pull changes with `sync?sinceRev=`, so new messages, edits, deletes and offline catch-up all use one path.
- **Live updates:** these ride the existing WebSocket (`ChatChanged`) and FCM doorbell.
- **Clients:** a shared `ChatController` in relay-client drives both apps.

**Tech Stack:** Ktor 3.3 + SQLite (`Store.kt`), kotlinx.serialization, relay-client `JsonFileStore`, Compose Desktop, Jetpack Compose, WorkManager.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-21-space-chats-design.md`. The approved mockups are the design-board artboards `DesktopChats`, `DesktopChatInvite`, `PhoneChatList` and `PhoneChat` (https://claude.ai/artifact/1icxaRRXueAkghY1UxXpa3). This plan is text only; media and video come in the next two plans.

## Global Constraints
- **Scope and visibility:**
  - A chat belongs to exactly one space.
  - Names are 1–40 characters with no control characters, using the same rule as space names (`validSpaceName`).
  - Only chat members and that space's admin can see that a chat exists: its name, members, messages, invites, events and errors. Everyone else gets 404 `not_found`.
- **Joining:**
  - Joining = invite (by any member) → owner approval (skipped when the owner invites) → the invitee's Join.
  - States are `PENDING_APPROVAL`, `APPROVED`, `DENIED`, `DECLINED`.
  - An invite expires 7 days after creation, or when the invitee or the inviter is no longer in the space.
  - An invite is only possible for a space member who is neither a chat member nor already invited.
- **Ownership:**
  - The owner is the creator. `POST owner` hands ownership to another member.
  - The owner gets 409 `owner_must_transfer` when trying to leave.
  - When the owner is removed from the space, ownership passes to the longest-standing member (earliest `joined_at`).
  - When the last member leaves, the chat is deleted.
- **Space admin:**
  - Sees and reads every chat in their space.
  - Can rename, remove members, delete any message, delete the chat, and join directly with no approval.
  - Cannot edit other people's text.
  - Moderation of other people's messages by a non-member admin shows as `Removed by the space admin` (member) or `Deleted by the space admin` (message).
- **Delete permissions:**
  - Delete chat: the owner or the space admin.
  - Edit message: the author only. Editing sets `editedAt`.
  - Delete message: the author or the space admin. A deleted message keeps its row with `deleted = true` and no body, and renders as `Message deleted`.
- **Space changes:** deleting a space deletes its chats. Removal from a space removes the device from that space's chats and from their invites.
- **Guests:** a guest device can never be in a chat.
- **Exact copy:**
  - `{inviter} wants to add {name}`, with `Approve` / `Deny`
  - `You're invited to {chat}`, with `Join` / `Decline`
  - `New chat…` (PC) / `New chat` (phone)
  - `Invite…`, `Make owner`, `Rename chat`, `Leave chat`, `Delete chat`
  - `(edited)`, `Message deleted`, `Deleted by the space admin`, `Removed by the space admin`
  - `admin view`
  - `Message {chat}` (composer placeholder)
  - Notification `{name} in {chat}: {text}`
  - PC ⋯ menu: `Copy link`, `Edit`, `Delete`. Phone long-press: `Share…`, `Edit`, `Delete`. (Download comes in the media plan.)
- **Sync:** `rev` is per chat and strictly increasing. `sync` returns every message with `rev > sinceRev`, including edited and deleted ones, plus the members, the owner and the current `rev`.
- **Limits:** message text is at most 4,000 characters; 400 `too_long` above that.
- **DB upgrade:** additive and in place, using `addColumnIfMissing` and `CREATE TABLE IF NOT EXISTS`, with an upgrade test from an old database.
- **Build:** `./gradlew check :android:lintDebug :android:assembleDebug` stays green.

## Review Focus
1. **A device removed from the space while it has a chat open.** Its next sync or post gets 404, and the client drops the chat locally with no crash and no lingering cache. Tested in C2 (`removedFromSpaceLosesChats`) and C3 (`syncNotFoundForgetsChat`).
2. **Two edits or deletes racing on one message.** `rev` stays strictly increasing and the last write wins. No rev is ever reused. Tested in C1 (`revStrictlyIncreasesUnderConcurrentWrites`).
3. **A very long or multi-line message with emoji and links.** It is stored and returned unchanged, and links are detected without breaking on punctuation. Tested in C2 (`longUnicodeTextRoundTrips`) and C3 (`linkifyHandlesTrailingPunctuation`).
4. **An invite left pending when the inviter leaves the space.** It disappears for the owner and the invitee, so nobody can approve or join a stale invite. Tested in C2 (`inviteDiesWhenInviterLeavesSpace`).
5. **Offline phone catch-up after 500+ messages.** Sync pages through all of them (page size 200, `hasMore`) with no gap and no duplicates. Tested in C2 (`syncPagesLargeBacklog`) and C3 (`syncFollowsHasMore`).

**Interfaces — Produces (Store):**
- Tables: `chats`, `chat_members`, `chat_invites` and `chat_messages`, laid out as in the spec. Add `chat_members.removed_by_admin` (INTEGER), plus indexes on `chat_messages(chat_id, rev)` and `chat_members(device_id)`.
- Chat lifecycle:
  - `createChat(spaceId, ownerId, name, nowMs): ChatRow`
  - `chat(id): ChatRow?`
  - `chatsOf(spaceId, deviceId, isAdmin: Boolean): List<ChatRow>`
  - `isChatMember(chatId, deviceId): Boolean`
  - `chatMembers(chatId): List<DeviceRow>` (ordered by `joined_at`)
  - `renameChat`
  - `deleteChat(id): List<String>` (returns the member ids to notify)
- Membership and invites:
  - `addChatMember`
  - `removeChatMember(chatId, deviceId, byAdmin): RemoveResult { REMOVED, NOT_MEMBER, OWNER_MUST_TRANSFER, LAST_MEMBER_DELETED }`
  - `transferChatOwner(chatId, from, to): Boolean`
  - `createInvite(chatId, inviteeId, inviterId, nowMs): ChatInviteRow?` (returns null if the invitee is already a member or already invited)
  - `invitesFor(deviceId, nowMs): List<ChatInviteRow>` (actionable invites: owner approvals plus your APPROVED invitations, excluding expired or stale ones)
  - `setInviteState(id, from: InviteState, to: InviteState): Boolean`
  - `joinViaInvite(id, deviceId, nowMs): Boolean`
- Messages and sync:
  - `postMessage(chatId, authorId, text, nowMs): ChatMessageRow` (bumps `chats.rev`)
  - `editMessage(id, authorId, text, nowMs): Boolean`
  - `deleteMessage(id, byDeviceId, byAdmin): Boolean`
  - `sync(chatId, sinceRev, limit = 200): Pair<List<ChatMessageRow>, Boolean>`
  - `markRead(chatId, deviceId, rev)`
- Space hook: `onLeftSpace(spaceId, deviceId)` removes the device from that space's chats, transfers ownership where needed, deletes chats left empty, and deletes its invites. Call it from the existing `removeMember` and `deleteSpace` paths, or cascade in their transactions.

Each mutating method is `@Synchronized` and runs in a transaction. `rev` comes from an `UPDATE chats SET rev = rev + 1 … RETURNING rev`-equivalent inside the same transaction.

**Interfaces — Produces (all authenticated; errors as `ErrorResponse`):**
- **Chats:**
  - `GET /v1/spaces/{id}/chats` → `List<ChatSummary>`. Members see their own chats; the admin sees all, with `member` set. A non-member of the space gets 404.
  - `POST /v1/spaces/{id}/chats` with `CreateChatRequest` → 201 `ChatSummary`
  - `PATCH /v1/chats/{id}` with `RenameChatRequest`: the owner or the admin
  - `DELETE /v1/chats/{id}`: the owner or the admin. Emits `ChatDeleted` to the members.
- **Invites:**
  - `POST /v1/chats/{id}/invites` with `InviteRequest`: any member. Errors: 400 `not_in_space`, 409 `already_member` or `already_invited`. An invite by the owner starts `APPROVED`.
  - `GET /v1/chat-invites` → `List<ChatInvite>` (actionable invites only)
  - `POST /v1/chat-invites/{id}/approve` and `/deny`: the owner only
  - `POST /v1/chat-invites/{id}/join` and `/decline`: the invitee only, and only when `APPROVED`
  - Every invite change emits `ChatInvitesChanged` to the owner, the inviter and the invitee.
- **Membership:**
  - `POST /v1/chats/{id}/owner` with `TransferOwnerRequest`: the owner; the target must be a member
  - `DELETE /v1/chats/{id}/members/{deviceId}`: yourself (leave) or the admin (remove)
  - `POST /v1/chats/{id}/join`: the space admin only
- **Messages:**
  - `GET /v1/chats/{id}/sync?sinceRev=N` → `ChatSync`: members and the admin
  - `POST /v1/chats/{id}/messages` with `PostMessageRequest` → 201 `ChatMessage`: members only. The admin must join first, else 403 `not_member`.
  - `PATCH /v1/chats/{id}/messages/{msgId}` with `EditMessageRequest`: the author only, else 403 `not_author`
  - `DELETE /v1/chats/{id}/messages/{msgId}`: the author, or the admin (sets `deletedByAdmin`)
  - `POST /v1/chats/{id}/read` with `ReadRequest`
- **Fan-out:** every message change emits `ChatChanged(chatId, rev)` over `SessionHub` to the members and the space admin, and pings the push tokens of members other than the actor (using the existing `Push` sender).
- **Visibility:** a device that is neither a chat member nor the admin gets 404 `not_found` on every chat route, so a chat's existence never leaks.

- **`linkify(text: String): List<TextPart>`**, where `TextPart` is either `Plain(String)` or `Link(url)`. It reuses the protocol `extractUrls` trimming rules.
- **Copy in `Messages`:** `chatNotification(name, chat, text)` returns `"{name} in {chat}: {text}"`, plus the constants listed in the Global Constraints.
